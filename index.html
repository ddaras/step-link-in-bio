What is Step? +

Step is a product concept for a simpler link-in-bio page: one focused destination that helps visitors see the next action you’d like them to take. We’re gathering interest while we build toward it.

Is Step available to use now? +

Not yet. The page shown here is an illustrative preview of the vision, not a live product. Join the waitlist to hear about future updates once signup storage is connected.

How much will it cost? +

Pricing hasn’t been announced. We’ll share details as the product takes shape, without making promises before they’re ready.

What happens when I join the waitlist? +

If the waitlist database is connected, we’ll store your email address and the time you signed up for early access updates. If it isn’t connected, the form will tell you your address wasn’t saved.
